The programme · Completed study

Phase 1 study

A study in {{fact:phase1.n}} healthy volunteers of how the body absorbs and handles {{fact:product.name}} after single doses, and of short-term tolerability. It was not a study of whether {{fact:product.name}} works.

Study design, doses and comparator

Each participant received three treatments, one in each of three periods, in a randomly assigned order (a crossover design, so each person acts as their own comparison). Participants knew which treatment they received (open-label).

  1. {{fact:product.name}} 600 mg: a single dose by mouth.
  2. {{fact:product.name}} 900 mg: a single dose by mouth.
  3. Epidyolex: a licensed cannabidiol oral solution, as the comparator.

There were {{fact:phase1.interval}}, with {{fact:phase1.sampling}}. Taking part lasted {{fact:phase1.length}}. Study procedures included a standardised high-fat meal, because food changes how much cannabidiol is absorbed. {{cite:perucca-2020}}
