In plain language

Clinical high risk of psychosis is well described: most people who meet the criteria do not develop psychosis, and {{fact:evidence.transition_3y}} do within three years. {{cite:salazar-2021}} Psychological therapy is the recommended first step, but no treatment has been proven to prevent psychosis. {{cite:davies-2018}}

Research on cannabidiol (CBD) in psychosis is early: small, short studies of other CBD products, with mixed results. None of them used {{fact:product.name}}. Evidence about {{fact:product.name}} itself so far comes only from a Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers, whose results are not yet published.
