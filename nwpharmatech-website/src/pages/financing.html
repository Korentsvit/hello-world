Programme financing

Programme financing

We are developing a way for eligible investors to help finance the NWPT-SM32300 programme. This page explains the approach in outline. It is not an offer, and no investment is being accepted.

Not an offer or invitation to invest

Nothing on this website is an offer, invitation or inducement to buy or sell any security, token or other financial product, in any jurisdiction. Any future offer would be made only through formal offering documents, only to people legally eligible to receive them, and only where lawful. The approach described here has not been approved by any regulator and may change or not go ahead.

The approach we are developing

Programme information

Preferred: platform-integrated programme financing. Eligible investors would participate in a dedicated programme vehicle that holds the economic interest in NWPT-SM32300, through a regulated investor platform that handles identity, eligibility and anti-money-laundering checks. At closing, investors' interests could potentially be issued as permissioned tokens.

Fallback: register-first. If a token at closing is not appropriate, interests would be recorded on a conventional register of holders. Any digital representation would follow only if and when it is lawful and approved.

Eligibility would be set by law in each country. It is likely to be limited to professional, qualified or accredited investors, and some countries will be excluded.

Terms used

Programme vehicle: a separate company or entity set up to finance one programme.

Permissioned token: a digital record of an interest that only verified, eligible holders can hold or receive. It is not freely tradable.

Register: the legal record of who holds an interest.

Financing does not influence the science

  • Investors and token holders have no say in study design, eligibility, dosing, safety decisions, analysis or publication.
  • Participant data are never shared with investors.
  • Results are reported whatever they show.
  • We do not seek investment from study participants or their families, and recruitment and clinical channels are never used to promote financing.

Risk, in brief

Any future opportunity would be high risk. NWPT-SM32300 may not work; most investigational medicines are never approved; money invested could be lost entirely; interests may be impossible to sell; and digital-asset and securities rules differ between countries and are changing.
