Funding and financing

How the programme is paid for, who decides how money is spent, and how we report progress. Nothing on this page is an offer, and no investment is being accepted through this website or through the financing route described here.

How the programme could be funded

Proposed
{{block:dao_image:09-dao-bounded-governance-hero.wide}}

Preferred: platform-integrated programme financing. Eligible investors would take part in a dedicated programme vehicle through a regulated investor platform, which would carry out eligibility, identity and anti-money-laundering checks. Where feasible, investors' interests would be issued as permissioned tokens at closing.

Fallback: register-first. Interests would be recorded on a conventional register of holders, with any digital representation added only if and when lawful and approved.

Eligibility would be set by the law of each country, is likely to be limited to professional, qualified or accredited investors, and would exclude some countries.

Who does what

Five groups would be involved. Two exist now. Three are proposed, and would exist only if a financing process goes ahead.

Public research community Exists now

Anyone can read the programme's science, evidence and updates, and send questions or corrections. Following or discussing the programme gives no right to invest, no claim to any token and no say in clinical decisions.

Eligible participants in a potential private financing process Proposed

If a financing process goes ahead, it would be private and open only to investors who meet the legal requirements of their country and pass the platform's checks. It would run on private legal documents, not on this website. Financing participants would not vote on clinical decisions.

Programme vehicle and legal register Proposed

A dedicated programme vehicle would hold the money raised for the programme, separately from other business. A conventional legal register would record who holds which interest, and would be the definitive record. Both are proposals: no investment is being accepted through them.

Possible permissioned digital representation Proposed

Where feasible, interests recorded in the register could also be issued as permissioned digital tokens at closing, held only by verified, eligible holders. A token would mirror the register and could not create rights on its own; if the two ever differed, the register would control. If this is not feasible, the register alone would be used.

Clinical and regulatory responsibilities Exists now

The study sponsor, the investigators, research ethics committees and the medicines regulator each have their own legal duties for a clinical study, set out under programme governance. None of these decisions is made by the community or by financing participants, and no funding milestone can authorise one.

What financing does not change

  • Following the programme creates no investment entitlement.
  • Financing participants would not vote on clinical decisions.
  • A funding milestone can release money for research work; it cannot approve or change anything clinical, and it is not a payment to investors.

How the parts relate

Proposed structure

The same, in words

  1. The public can read the programme's science and updates here and send questions. Nothing on this website takes investment or payment.
  2. If financing goes ahead, eligible investors would be checked by a regulated platform and sign private documents with a dedicated programme vehicle. The vehicle's legal register would be the definitive record of who holds what; a permissioned digital record might mirror it, and the register would control if they differed.
  3. The vehicle would release money in stages, against operational milestones, to pay for research work.
  4. Clinical decisions stay with the sponsor, investigators, ethics committees and regulator, each under its own legal duties. No funding milestone, investor or community member can authorise or change them.

The register and any digital record

Proposed

Under the proposal, the legal register of the programme vehicle would be the definitive record of each investor's interest, whether or not a digital record exists. A permissioned digital record, if used, would be derived from the register: it would be issued only to verified, eligible holders and could not create or change rights by itself.

If the register and the digital record ever differed, the register would control. There is no public sale, trading or wallet connection, and none is planned on this website.

Use of funds

Funding would support:

  • Running clinical studies, including the planned Phase 2B study
  • Manufacturing clinical supplies of {{fact:product.name}} to GMP standard
  • Regulatory, safety and quality work
  • Programme operations, data management and analysis

Proposed controls over research spending Proposed

Money raised through programme financing would be kept for the programme and released to it in stages, as defined operational milestones are met. A milestone releases money for research work. It does not approve any clinical step, which needs its own independent approvals, and it is not a payment to investors.

What is and is not funded

{{block:milestones:funding}}

Figures are published only once they are approved and can be shown as one of: planning target, committed funding, cash received, or money spent. Expressions of interest are never counted as funding.

Who controls spending and clinical decisions today

Spending is controlled by the board of {{fact:company.name}}, the programme's company.

Clinical decisions, including study design, eligibility, dosing and safety, are kept independent of funding and financing. Each study needs approval from a medicines regulator and a research ethics committee before it can start.

How progress is reported

Dated events appear on the updates page, and study milestones with their sources on the study page. No programme reports have been published.

Updates on this website are not investment communications.
