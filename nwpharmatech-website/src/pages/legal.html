Accessibility

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. The site supports keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen readers, text resizing to 200% and use without JavaScript. Automated checks (axe-core) report no violations on any page; a full manual audit, including with assistive technology users, has not yet been carried out.

If you find anything difficult to use, please contact us.
