Programme brief

The programme on one page

NWPharmaTech presents our research programme for people at clinical high risk of psychosis, explaining the clinical need, the science and the people behind it.

The programme centres on {{fact:product.name}}, an investigational oral cannabidiol formulation, and a planned Phase 2B study evaluating {{fact:phase2b.aims}}.

Alongside the clinical programme, we are developing a financing route through which eligible investors could take part through a dedicated programme vehicle, potentially using permissioned tokens. Clinical decisions remain independent of financing, and the treatment's effectiveness is unproven.

We are developing platform-integrated programme financing through a dedicated programme vehicle, potentially with permissioned tokens issued at closing where feasible. No investment is being accepted, and financing has no influence on clinical decisions.
