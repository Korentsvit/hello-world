Programme brief

What this programme is, in one page

NWPharmaTech presents our research programme for people at clinical high risk of psychosis, explaining the clinical need, the science and the people behind it.

The programme centres on NWPT-SM32300, an investigational oral cannabidiol formulation, and a planned Phase 2B study evaluating dose response, symptoms, safety and tolerability. A Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers has been completed.

Alongside the clinical programme, we are developing a financing route through which eligible investors could take part through a dedicated programme vehicle, potentially using permissioned tokens. Clinical decisions remain independent of financing, and the treatment's effectiveness is unproven.

How we label evidence

Emerging evidence

Programme information

Open question

Independent clinical decisions

Study design, participant safety, data analysis and publication are decided by the clinical and scientific leadership, subject to regulators and research ethics committees. Funders and investors have no say in them.

We intend to register every study before it starts and to report results whatever they show. Phase 1 is registered as NCT07186283.

Important

NWPT-SM32300 is not approved by any medicines regulator. Cannabidiol products sold in shops or online are different products and have not been shown to prevent psychosis.
