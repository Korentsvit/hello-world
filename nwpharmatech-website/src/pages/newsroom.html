How we report progress

Each programme report is written to answer four questions:

  1. What changed? Events since the last report, with dates.
  2. What supports it? The record or source for each statement.
  3. What remains unresolved? Open questions, delays and risks.
  4. What happens next? The next steps, marked as planned.

Individual events are listed on the updates page as they happen.
