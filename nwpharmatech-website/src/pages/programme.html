Why a micellar softgel, and why study a range of doses

What "Phase 2B" means

Medicines are tested in stages. Phase 1 studies look mainly at safety and how the body handles a medicine, usually in healthy volunteers. Phase 2 studies look at whether it may work in people with the condition, and at what dose.

A Phase 2B study typically compares more than one dose with a control, to understand the dose–response relationship and decide whether a larger Phase 3 study is justified.

Protecting participants

The Phase 2B study can start only after approval by a research ethics committee and the relevant medicines regulators. Anyone taking part would give informed consent and could leave at any time. Cannabidiol can raise liver enzymes and interact with other medicines, {{cite:epidyolex-smpc}} so safety monitoring will be set out in the study protocol.

Can I take part?

Not yet: the Phase 2B study is not recruiting, and we cannot supply {{fact:product.name}} outside an approved study.
