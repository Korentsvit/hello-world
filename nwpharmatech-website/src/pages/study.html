Public synopsis

Programme information

The planned Phase 2B study will evaluate {{fact:phase2b.aims}} of {{fact:product.name}} in {{fact:phase2b.population}}. It follows a completed Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers, which looked at how the body absorbs and handles the medicine and at short-term tolerability.

The Phase 2B study is not recruiting. It will start only after regulatory and research ethics approval, and will be registered publicly before anyone is enrolled.

Registrations

The completed Phase 1 study is registered on two public registries. Both entries describe the same study: a single-dose, three-period crossover study of NW300EMCBD (the development code for {{fact:product.name}}) in healthy adults aged 18 to 55, sponsored by {{fact:company.name}}.

Sponsor and oversight

In clinical trial law, the sponsor is the organisation that takes responsibility for starting, managing and financing a study. {{fact:company.name}} was the sponsor of the Phase 1 study. The sponsor of the Phase 2B study will be named in its registry entry.

Publication policy

Draft for management adoption

This is a proposed policy. It has not been adopted, and no timetable in it is yet a commitment.

  1. Disclosure regardless of outcome. Results of every study of {{fact:product.name}} would be made public whether they are positive, negative or inconclusive, including studies stopped early.
  2. Registry results. Summary results would be posted on each registry where the study is registered, within the period the registry or applicable law requires.
  3. Plain-language summary. A plain-language summary would be published on this site and offered to participants, reviewed for accuracy and readability before release.
  4. Journal submission. Full results of each Phase 2 or later study would be submitted to a peer-reviewed journal, with authorship decided by recognised authorship criteria and no funder or investor right to delay or alter the manuscript.
  5. Timing. The proposed timings for each step are to be set by management when the policy is adopted.
