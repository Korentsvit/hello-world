The financing route under evaluation Proposed

Preferred path under evaluation: platform-integrated programme financing. Eligible investors would take part in a dedicated programme vehicle through a regulated investor platform, which would carry out eligibility, identity and anti-money-laundering checks. Where feasible, their interests would be issued as permissioned tokens at closing.

Fallback: register-first. A conventional private placement, with interests recorded on a legal investor register and any digital representation added only if and when lawful and approved.

These are development objectives, not live offering terms.

Who does what

Public research community Exists now

Anyone can follow the programme and send questions or corrections. Following or discussing it gives no right to invest, no claim to any token and no say in clinical decisions.

Eligible participants in a potential private financing process Proposed

If financing goes ahead, it would be private and open only to investors who meet the legal requirements of their country and pass the platform's checks. It would run on private legal documents, not on this website. Financing participants would not vote on clinical decisions.

Programme vehicle and legal register Proposed

A dedicated programme vehicle would hold money raised for the programme, separately from other business. Its legal register would be the definitive record of who holds which interest. No investment is being accepted through either.

Possible permissioned digital representation Proposed

Where feasible, interests on the register could also be issued as permissioned tokens at closing, held only by verified, eligible holders. A token would mirror the register and could not create rights on its own. If this is not feasible, the register alone would be used.

Clinical and regulatory responsibilities Exists now

The sponsor, investigators, research ethics committees and the medicines regulator each have their own legal duties for a clinical study (see programme governance). None of these decisions is made by the community or by financing participants, and no funding milestone can authorise one.

How the parts relate

Public information and clinical responsibilities exist now; the private financing route is a proposal. Money raised could pay for research work, but the financing side has no part in clinical decisions.

The register and any digital record

The programme vehicle's legal register would be the definitive record of each investor's interest, whether or not a digital record exists. A permissioned digital record, if used, would be derived from the register, issued only to verified, eligible holders, and could not create or change rights by itself.

If the register and a digital record ever differed, the register would control. There is no public sale, trading or wallet connection.

Use of funds Proposed controls

Funding would support clinical studies, including the planned Phase 2B study; GMP manufacture of {{fact:product.name}} for those studies; regulatory, safety and quality work; and programme operations, data management and analysis.

Money raised through programme financing would be kept for the programme and released to it in stages, as defined milestones are met. A milestone releases money for research work; it approves nothing clinical and is not a payment to investors.

No funding figures have been published. Figures are published only once approved, labelled as a planning target, committed funding, cash received or money spent; expressions of interest are never counted.
