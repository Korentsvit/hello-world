How clinical decisions stay independent

Because the programme is exploring new ways to finance research, it is especially important that funding cannot influence the science. Our governance is being designed so that:

  • decisions on study design, participant eligibility, safety and conduct are made by the clinical and scientific leadership, subject to approval by regulators and research ethics committees;
  • an independent Data Monitoring Committee reviews accumulating safety data and can recommend changes to, or stopping of, the study;
  • the statistical analysis plan is finalised before the data are unblinded;
  • the results will be reported whatever they show;
  • investors and token holders, if any, have no rights to influence clinical decisions, access participant data, or delay or shape publications.
