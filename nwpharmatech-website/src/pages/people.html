How clinical decisions stay independent

Because the programme is exploring new ways to finance research, it matters that funding cannot influence the science.

  • Decisions on study design, eligibility, safety and conduct are made by the clinical and scientific leadership, and approved by regulators and research ethics committees.
  • The statistical analysis plan is finalised before study data are unblinded.
  • Results are reported whatever they show.
  • Investors and token holders have no right to influence clinical decisions, see participant data, or delay or shape publications.
