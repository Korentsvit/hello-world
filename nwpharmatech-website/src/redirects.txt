# Page URLs are extensionless: Cloudflare Pages serves /study from study.html and itself answers
# /study.html and /study/ with a 308 to /study, and /index.html with a 308 to /. Never add a rule for a
# page's own route or those forms: a rule such as "/study /study.html" loops with Cloudflare's 308.
# Every target must be a page route (optionally with a #fragment); a target must not itself be redirected.
