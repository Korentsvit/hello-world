Preparing for an appointment

Fill this in by hand and take it with you. You do not have to answer everything. Nothing on this sheet is sent to anyone unless you choose to share it.

What I have noticed

Experiences, when they started, how often, how much they affect me.

What makes things better or worse

For example sleep, stress, alcohol or drugs, time with others.

How daily life is going

School, college or work; friends and family; sleep; appetite.

Medicines and other treatment

Including anything bought without a prescription, such as CBD products or supplements.

My questions

  • What did the assessment find, and what does it mean for me?
  • What support can you offer, and how soon?
  • Who do I contact between appointments, and what if things get worse?
