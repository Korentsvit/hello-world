NWPharmaTech presents our research programme for people at clinical high risk of psychosis, explaining the clinical need, the science and the people behind it.

The programme centres on {{fact:product.name}}, an investigational oral cannabidiol formulation, and a planned Phase 2B study evaluating {{fact:phase2b.aims}}. The website brings together accessible explanations, supporting evidence and programme updates, clearly distinguishing established findings from questions our research aims to answer.

Alongside the clinical programme, we are developing a financing route through which eligible investors, including those in the crypto and mental-health communities, could participate through a dedicated programme vehicle, potentially using permissioned tokens. Our ambition is to connect rigorous pharmaceutical development with transparent communication and broader access to research financing, while keeping clinical decisions independent and recognising that the effectiveness of {{fact:product.name}} remains unproven.

What is established, and what is not

  • Established: in pooled studies, {{fact:evidence.transition_3y}} people at clinical high risk developed psychosis within three years; no medicine is licensed for this group; NICE advises against antipsychotics for prevention.
  • Emerging: small, short studies of other CBD preparations in schizophrenia (mixed results) and in clinical high risk (brain activity, stress response, and one 21-day symptom study).
  • Open: whether {{fact:product.name}} reduces symptoms, which dose is best, and its safety and tolerability in this population.
