What is established, and what is not

  • Established: in an updated meta-analysis, the pooled estimate was that {{fact:evidence.transition_3y}} people at clinical high risk developed psychosis within three years, and most do not; no medicine is licensed for this group; NICE advises against antipsychotics for prevention.
  • Emerging: small, short studies of other CBD preparations in schizophrenia (mixed results) and in clinical high risk (brain activity, stress response, and one 21-day symptom study).
  • Open: whether {{fact:product.name}} reduces symptoms, which dose is best, and its safety and tolerability in this population.
