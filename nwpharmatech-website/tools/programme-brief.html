Programme brief

Version: staging draft, 23 September 2026

NWPharmaTech presents our research programme for people at clinical high risk of psychosis, explaining the clinical need, the science and the people behind it.

The programme centres on NWPT-SM32300, an investigational oral cannabidiol formulation, and a planned Phase 2B study evaluating dose response, symptoms, safety and tolerability. The website brings together accessible explanations, supporting evidence and programme updates, clearly distinguishing established findings from questions our research aims to answer.

Alongside the clinical programme, we are developing a financing route through which eligible investors, including those in the crypto and mental-health communities, could participate through a dedicated programme vehicle, potentially using permissioned tokens. Our ambition is to connect rigorous pharmaceutical development with transparent communication and broader access to research financing, while keeping clinical decisions independent and recognising that the treatment's effectiveness remains unproven.

Investigational medicine. NWPT-SM32300 is not approved for any use and its effectiveness is unproven. This brief is not medical advice and is not an offer of any security or token.

Where the programme stands

NWPT-SM32300Oral softgel, 300 mg highly purified cannabidiol, self-emulsifying formulation; GMP manufacture
Phase 1 (completed)Healthy volunteers (n=14), randomised crossover vs Epidyolex, single doses of 600 mg and 900 mg; UK; NCT07186283 / ISRCTN25163383; database locked March 2026; results summary to follow review
Phase 2B (planned)People at clinical high risk of psychosis; aims: dose response, symptoms, safety, tolerability; protocol being finalised; not recruiting
FinancingIn development: platform-integrated programme financing via a dedicated vehicle, potentially with a permissioned token at closing; register-first fallback. No investment accepted

What is established, and what is not

  • Established: about one in four people at clinical high risk develop psychosis within three years; no medicine is licensed for this group; NICE advises against antipsychotics for prevention.
  • Emerging: small, short studies of other CBD preparations in schizophrenia (mixed results) and in clinical high risk (brain activity, stress response, one 21-day symptom study).
  • Open: whether NWPT-SM32300 reduces symptoms, which dose is best, and its safety and tolerability in this population.
