# Module mapping

One entry per module: what it adds to the live site, where it belongs, which live content it overlaps, and the source checks that remain. The live site could not be read from the build environment (its address is blocked by the environment's network policy), so overlaps name the kind of live content to compare, not quoted text. Generated by `python3 build.py`; the checks are the manifests' `publication_blockers`.

The Programme Room is a separate component that is not part of this package; no module depends on it.

## Study progress (`study-hub`)

- **Adds:** Where the programme stands: a dated summary (aim, current stage, completed work, next milestone), a sourced milestone table (Phase 1 done; Phase 2B protocol in progress), both registry entries for Phase 1, sponsor and oversight facts, and the programme brief PDF.
- **Belongs:** Its own page (default /study), or a 'Progress' section of the live programme or pipeline page. Default path `/study`.
- **Overlaps:** Any live 'pipeline', 'programme' or 'clinical development' section, and above all the live site's published proposed CHR-P design. Keep that design section: this module states only population, aims and status. If the live page already has a progress timeline, use the milestone table in its place only after the dates are compared.
- **Not included:** Doses, comparator, duration, endpoints or size for Phase 2B.
- **Files:** `fragment.html`, `module.css`; script: none; downloads: `downloads/nwpharmatech-programme-brief.pdf`.
- **Depends on:** none.
- **Source checks remaining:**
  - primary-source check of 0 reference(s) and 1 fact(s) verified so far only through search summaries or registry indexes (`company.name`)
  - compare its Phase 2B statements with the live published CHR-P design (`clinical_design` in the manifest; `CLINICAL-DESIGN-CHECK.md`)
  - board and legal approval of the funding and financing wording before publication, including whether the brief should again name investor audiences as management's original brief did ('including those in the crypto and mental-health communities'; removed here so that the brief matches the homepage)
  - reconcile the Phase 1 start date: company records give first consent on 27 August 2025, ClinicalTrials.gov gives a study start of 22 September 2025
  - confirm that 'Protocol being finalised' is still the Phase 2B status (last documented 23 February 2026), and give the next milestone date if one is set

## Phase 1 study (`phase-1`)

- **Adds:** The completed healthy-volunteer study: who took part, design, doses (single 600 mg and 900 mg; Epidyolex comparator), what was measured, timeline, registrations, and results status (not published).
- **Belongs:** Its own page (default /phase-1), linked from the pipeline or study page. Default path `/phase-1`.
- **Overlaps:** Any live Phase 1 or 'completed study' summary or news item. The doses here are Phase 1 single doses in healthy adults; they are not the Phase 2B dose range.
- **Not included:** Phase 1 results (not published until reconciled).
- **Files:** `fragment.html`, `module.css`; script: none; downloads: none.
- **Depends on:** `evidence-library` (link target); `study-hub` (link target).
- **Source checks remaining:**
  - primary-source check of 1 reference(s) and 6 fact(s) verified so far only through search summaries or registry indexes (`perucca-2020`, `phase1.isrctn`, `phase1.isrctn_url`, `phase1.location`, `phase1.nct`, `phase1.nct_url`, `phase1.status`)
  - compare its Phase 2B statements with the live published CHR-P design (`clinical_design` in the manifest; `CLINICAL-DESIGN-CHECK.md`)

## Formulation explanation (`formulation`)

- **Adds:** Why an oral CBD formulation matters: low and variable absorption of oral CBD, the effect of food, and what the self-emulsifying softgel is designed to change, with an accessible diagram description.
- **Belongs:** A section of the live science, technology or product page (default /science#formulation). Default path `/science#formulation`.
- **Overlaps:** Any live 'technology', 'formulation' or 'our product' copy. It makes no claim of bioequivalence, superior tolerability or benefit; if live copy does, that is a separate review.
- **Not included:** The softgel render (optional asset, not supplied); comparative claims.
- **Files:** `fragment.html`, `module.css`; script: none; downloads: none.
- **Depends on:** `evidence-library` (link target); `phase-1` (link target).
- **Source checks remaining:**
  - primary-source check of 2 reference(s) and 0 fact(s) verified so far only through search summaries or registry indexes (`perucca-2020`, `taylor-2018`)

## Guide for young people and families (`family-guide`)

- **Adds:** Plain-language explanation of clinical high risk, what to do and where to go by UK nation, Ireland and the US, and the appointment preparation sheet (PDF).
- **Belongs:** Its own page (default /families), reachable from patient-facing navigation. Default path `/families`.
- **Overlaps:** Any live 'patients', 'families' or 'support' page and the live site's urgent-help or crisis information. It links to an urgent-help page, which must exist on the live site (dependency 'urgent-help').
- **Not included:** Symptom quiz, risk score, diagnosis, health-data collection or investment prompts (none by design).
- **Files:** `fragment.html`, `module.css`; script: none; downloads: `downloads/appointment-preparation-sheet.pdf`.
- **Depends on:** `urgent-help` (blocks publication until checked); `evidence-library` (link target).
- **Source checks remaining:**
  - primary-source check of 3 reference(s) and 1 fact(s) verified so far only through search summaries or registry indexes (`fusarpoli-2013`, `nice-cg155`, `salazar-2021`, `evidence.transition_3y`)
  - live dependency `urgent-help`: /faq#urgent-help must reach a live page that gives urgent help by country: emergency numbers and crisis lines for the United Kingdom, Ireland and the United States, checked immediately before launch. Not confirmed on the live site (record the check in src/integration-routes.json -> confirmed_dependencies and rebuild)
