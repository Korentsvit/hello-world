About us

NWPharmaTech Ltd is a UK company developing NWPT-SM32300, an investigational oral cannabidiol medicine, for people at clinical high risk of psychosis. A Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers (NCT07186283) was completed on 10 December 2025; a Phase 2B study is planned. NWPT-SM32300 is not approved for any use and its effectiveness is unproven.

When writing about the programme

Please describe NWPT-SM32300 as investigational, and do not describe it as a treatment or cure. Results from other cannabidiol products are not results for NWPT-SM32300.

How we report progress

Each programme report is written to answer four questions:

  1. What changed? Events since the last report, with dates.
  2. What supports it? The record or source for each statement.
  3. What remains unresolved? Open questions, delays and risks.
  4. What happens next? The next steps, marked as planned.

Individual events are listed on the updates page as they happen.
