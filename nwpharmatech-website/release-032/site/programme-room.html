    Stage 1 · Clinical need · Background Documented

    Why people at clinical high risk are a research priority

    What this stage investigates

    What is known about people who meet criteria for clinical high risk of psychosis (CHR-P), and what care is recommended today.

    • CHR-P is not a diagnosis of psychosis or schizophrenia, and most people in this group do not develop psychosis.
    • An updated meta-analysis estimated that about one in four people identified as CHR-P (pooled estimate, 25%) developed psychosis within three years.
    • In England, psychological therapy is the recommended first step. NICE advises against antipsychotics to prevent psychosis in this group.
    • No preventive intervention has been shown to work better than the others.

  2. 2

    Stage 2 · Formulation · Completed 2025 Documented

    An oral softgel designed for a poorly water-soluble medicine

    What this stage investigates

    Whether a fixed-strength micellar softgel is a suitable way to give cannabidiol by mouth.

    • Cannabidiol dissolves poorly in water. Taken by mouth while fasting, only about 6% of a dose is estimated to reach the bloodstream, and food changes absorption markedly (research on other CBD products).
    • NWPT-SM32300 is a 300 mg softgel whose contents are designed to disperse into very small droplets in the gut.
    • The final formulation and GMP clinical supply were completed before Phase 1 dosing in 2025.

    Open question Whether this formulation gives more consistent absorption than other CBD products has not been published.

    Stage 3 · Phase 1 · Completed Dec 2025 Documented

    Phase 1 in healthy volunteers: absorption and short-term tolerability

    What this stage investigates

    How the body absorbs and handles NWPT-SM32300 after single doses, and short-term tolerability, compared with a licensed cannabidiol oral solution. It was not a study of whether NWPT-SM32300 works.

    • 14 healthy adult volunteers; randomised, open-label, three-period crossover.
    • Sponsor of the Phase 1 study: NWPharmaTech Ltd (as registered).
    • Results have not yet been published.

  5. 5

    Stage 5 · Phase 2B · Planned, no date Proposed

    The planned Phase 2B study in people at clinical high risk

    What this stage investigates

    Dose response, symptoms, safety and tolerability of NWPT-SM32300 in people at clinical high risk of psychosis, to inform the next stage of development.

    • Status: planned. Not recruiting.
    • Applicable approvals are not confirmed in public materials. Study initiation remains subject to funding, finalisation and approvals.
    • Oversight arrangements for the Phase 2B study have not yet been published.
    • A larger UK trial of cannabidiol in this group (CANTOP-RCT) was funded but never started, which is one reason this question remains open.
    • A small 21-day study of another CBD product in this group was published in 2024. Its findings are early, have not been confirmed in a larger trial, and do not apply directly to NWPT-SM32300.

    Stage 6 · Longer term · Future question Open question

    Could psychosis be delayed or prevented?

    What this stage investigates

    Whether transition to psychosis can be delayed or prevented. This is a longer-term question, not the current primary aim.

    • Answering it would need an adequately powered trial with long follow-up, if it were pursued as a primary objective.
    • Positive results on symptoms would not, by themselves, show prevention.

Open questions Open question

What the research does not yet know, and what would answer each question.

  • Does NWPT-SM32300 reduce symptoms in people at clinical high risk?

    Not established. It would take the results of a pre-specified clinical study such as the planned Phase 2B.

  • Is there a dose response, and which dose would be best?

    Not established. Dose response is one of the planned study’s aims.

  • Is it safe and well tolerated in this population?

    Not established. Phase 1 involved healthy volunteers and single doses only.

  • Does the formulation give more consistent absorption than other CBD products?

    Not published. Phase 1 measured absorption; its results are awaited.

  • How much reaches the brain?

    No study has measured it.

  • Can transition to psychosis be delayed or prevented?

    A longer-term question needing a separately powered trial with long follow-up.
