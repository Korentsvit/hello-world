Programme

Programme roadmap

Communication and website milestones are listed separately from clinical development milestones for the planned Phase 2B CHR-P study with NWPT-SM32300. Status labels are qualitative only — completed, in progress, planned, or not confirmed publicly. We do not invent completion percentages or funds raised.

Last reviewed: 22 September 2026

Website milestones are not clinical progress. Publishing science pages or a synopsis does not mean ethics or regulatory approvals are granted, sites are activated, or recruitment has begun. Investigational programme — effectiveness in this population has not been established. This website is not a trial enrolment portal.

A. Communication / website milestones

  • Completed

    Public science resources live

    Publish informational science, evidence, glossary and related pages for patients, families, clinicians and researchers.

    Evidence: Live pages on this site (Science, Evidence, Glossary and related)

  • Completed

    Programme synopsis / Phase 2B overview published

    Publish a public programme synopsis and Phase 2B overview describing the planned study at a high level.

    Evidence: Public synopsis and Phase 2B overview pages on this site

  • Completed

    Investigational formulation described

    Describe NWPT-SM32300 publicly as the investigational oral CBD micellar softgel candidate for the CHR-P programme.

    Evidence: Public product description on Science, Programme and public synopsis; investigational — effectiveness not established

B. Clinical development milestones (Phase 2B)

  • In progress

    Study preparation

    Finalise operational design for the proposed Phase 2B study (sites, synopsis lock, budget and operational package).

    Evidence: Working public synopsis and planning materials; full operational finalisation ongoing

  • Not confirmed publicly

    Applicable approvals

    Obtain ethics, regulatory and other applicable approvals required before site activation.

    Evidence: Not confirmed in public materials

  • Planned

    Site activation

    Contract and activate participating clinical sites under the approved protocol.

    Evidence: Depends on approvals and operational readiness

  • Planned

    Recruitment

    Enrol eligible participants into the Phase 2B study.

    Evidence: Follows site activation; this website is not an enrolment portal

  • Planned

    Follow-up

    Complete treatment and follow-up assessments per protocol (including week-16 follow-up in the working design).

    Evidence: Follows recruitment

  • Planned

    Analysis

    Analyse primary and secondary outcomes and report findings according to the statistical analysis plan.

    Evidence: Follows follow-up and database lock
