Clinical need and existing evidence

What published research says about people at clinical high risk of psychosis.

  • Finding NWPharmaTech public informationMatches your answers

    An updated meta-analysis estimated that about one in four people identified as CHR-P (pooled estimate, 25%) developed psychosis within three years.

    Related stage: Stage 1 · Clinical need — stage work status: Background

    Sources and limitations (1)

Formulation and absorption

What is known about giving cannabidiol by mouth, and about the NWPT-SM32300 softgel.

  • Finding NWPharmaTech public informationMatches your answers

    Cannabidiol dissolves poorly in water. Taken by mouth while fasting, only about 6% of a dose is estimated to reach the bloodstream, and food changes absorption markedly (research on other CBD products).

    Related stage: Stage 2 · Formulation — stage work status: Completed

    Sources and limitations (2)

    1. Perucca and Bialer — oral cannabidiol bioavailability (review), CNS Drugs

      Evidence library entry · Original source: DOI · Original source: PubMed

      Source type
      Published research
      Relates to
      Other CBD products — not NWPT-SM32300
      Source date
      Published 2020
      Limitations
      Review of other CBD preparations; figures vary between studies.

    2. Taylor et al. — Phase 1 study of purified CBD, including a food-effect arm, CNS Drugs

      Evidence library entry · Original source: DOI · Original source: PubMed

      Source type
      Published research
      Relates to
      Other CBD products — not NWPT-SM32300
      Source date
      Published 2018
      Limitations
      Healthy volunteers; a different CBD product. A published correction exists.

  • Finding NWPharmaTech public informationMatches your answers

    NWPT-SM32300 is a 300 mg softgel whose contents are designed to disperse into very small droplets in the gut.
