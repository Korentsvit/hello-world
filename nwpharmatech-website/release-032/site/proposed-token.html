Proposed token model

A short description of a proposed permissioned representation of contractual programme interests — and how it would sit inside the wider economic lifecycle. Exact economic terms, supply, and price are not published here. This is not an offer.

We are evaluating a platform-supported route for eligible investors to help finance the CHR-P research programme, including a permissioned digital record of participation at closing where that proves feasible. If a platform path cannot meet legal, operational or investor-access requirements, a conventional private placement with a legal investor register remains the fallback. These are development objectives, not live offering terms. Neither path is available on this website: production investment, payment and token issuance remain inactive. Community updates and discussion are separate from any regulated investment process.

Percentages, supply, and price are omitted. No mint / wallet / pay / subscribe UI on this website.

Last reviewed: 23 September 2026 · Proposed · Illustrative · Not an offer

Proposed summary

We are evaluating a platform-supported route for eligible investors to help finance the CHR-P research programme, including a permissioned digital record of participation at closing where that proves feasible. If a platform path cannot meet legal, operational or investor-access requirements, a conventional private placement with a legal investor register remains the fallback. These are development objectives, not live offering terms. Neither path is available on this website: production investment, payment and token issuance remain inactive. Community updates and discussion are separate from any regulated investment process.

If a private financing proceeds, eligible investors would hold contractual interests recorded on a conventional register under private legal documents — not on this website. Any permissioned digital representation would only mirror those register interests for verified holders. The register controls if token and register disagree.

Such a representation would not create medical voting rights, parent-company equity, ownership of the medicine or patient data, or public retail trading. Community membership on this site is not an investment entitlement and does not imply future tokens.

This website cannot accept investment. There is no mint, wallet connect, pay, or subscribe interface here. Percentages, token supply, and price are not published on this site. Any future figures would appear only after formal review and approval for public release.

Where a token would sit in the lifecycle

Proposed · Illustrative. Any permissioned digital unit under evaluation is not the financing itself. Capital, if raised, would enter a programme vehicle first. Ops milestones unlock spend, not distributions. Qualifying receipts, if any, may fund contractual distributions to register holders after expenses and reserves — and those distributions may be zero.

  1. Capital raised into a dedicated programme vehicle (private docs · not on this website)
  2. Programme funding released on operational milestones to NWPharmaTech as pharmaceutical sponsor
  3. Potential qualifying receipts in the defined CHR-P field — may never occur
  4. Contractual share → expenses / reserves → holder distributions to the register
  5. Permissioned digital unit (under evaluation) — if used, only mirrors register units; register wins on conflict

Rights the token would not create

Proposed.

  • No medical or clinical voting
  • No NWPharmaTech parent equity
  • No ownership of the medicine, core IP, or patient data
  • No public retail mint, DEX listing, or permissionless trading on this site
  • No economic rights from community membership alone

Transfers, if any digital unit exists, would only follow the register / allowlist process among legally eligible, KYC/AML-verified investors — not an open public market.
