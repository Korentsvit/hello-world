Community

Ways to take part

This initiative is for people who care about earlier intervention in psychosis — researchers, clinicians, journalists and supporters. Clinical decisions stay with NWPharmaTech and independent oversight. You do not need a crypto wallet, and participation is never a promise of tokens or investment allocations.

Available now / being developed

Available now Ways to take part today

  • Programme information — science, synopsis, evidence
  • Scenario explorer — educational narratives, not a calculation engine
  • Updates articles — programme news on the Updates hub
  • Contact — research, press, or financing enquiry (enquiry only)

Being developed Not live

We are evaluating a platform-supported route for eligible investors to help finance the CHR-P research programme, including a permissioned digital record of participation at closing where that proves feasible. If a platform path cannot meet legal, operational or investor-access requirements, a conventional private placement with a legal investor register remains the fallback. These are development objectives, not live offering terms. Neither path is available on this website: production investment, payment and token issuance remain inactive. Community updates and discussion are separate from any regulated investment process.

  • No eligibility decision, allocation, or liquidity is offered here
  • Membership or following updates is not an investment and creates no economic rights

What we do not ask you to do

  • Submit personal medical histories or use this site as a diagnosis tool
  • Invest or connect a wallet through these pages
  • Vote on clinical trial design
