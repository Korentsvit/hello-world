About us

NWPharmaTech Ltd is a UK company developing NWPT-SM32300, an investigational oral cannabidiol medicine, for people at clinical high risk of psychosis. A Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers (NCT07186283) was completed on 10 December 2025; a Phase 2B study is planned. NWPT-SM32300 is not approved for any use and its effectiveness is unproven.

When writing about the programme

Please describe NWPT-SM32300 as investigational, and do not describe it as a treatment or cure. Results from other cannabidiol products are not results for NWPT-SM32300.

Corrections log

  • 26 September 2026 — transition to psychosis (Salazar de Pablo et al., 2021, JAMA Psychiatry). The one-in-four figure (25% within three years) is the paper’s pooled estimate. It had been labelled a Kaplan–Meier estimate on the home page, the family guide, this evidence library and the clinical-need explainer. The paper’s separate Kaplan–Meier series (8% at six months, 14% at one year, 20% at two years, 27% at three years and 35% at ten years) is now shown as a separate analysis, and the two are not combined. The older pooled averages of Fusar-Poli et al., 2012 are unchanged. Details: evidence library.
