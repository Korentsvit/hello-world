Scientific leadership

Programme scientific leads for CHR-P and related psychiatric development. Individual scientific and company roles — not institutional endorsements of this website or any financing vehicle.

CHR-P programme lead

Professor Scott Woods

Individual scientific / programme leadership Individual capacity

Clinical high-risk-for-psychosis expertise supporting early identification, assessment, and intervention research.

More about this role

Yale Professor of Psychiatry and a recognised leader in CHR-P assessment instruments, including PSYCHS, SIPS, and Mini-SIPS. As CHR-P programme lead in an individual capacity, he guides scientific framing of assessment, enrichment, and intervention design for this workstream. NWPharmaTech remains pharmaceutical sponsor (CMC, safety, regulatory); investigators, ethics committees, and regulators remain independent.

Yale disclaimer: Professor Woods’s role is individual scientific and programme leadership. Yale University does not sponsor or endorse this website, the DAO, or any financing vehicle.

Dr Grace Blest-Hopley

Partner & Chief Scientific Officer

Company scientific leadership for NWPharmaTech’s research and development agenda, including the investigational CHR-P candidate.

More about this role

As Chief Scientific Officer, accountable for the company’s scientific agenda across research and development. For the CHR-P workstream this includes formulation characterisation and the scientific rationale for dose-response, symptom, safety and tolerability evaluation of investigational NWPT-SM32300. Effectiveness in this population has not been established.

Dr John Kane, MD

Lead — Schizophrenia Programme

Senior psychiatric leadership for the company’s schizophrenia development strategy and clinical programme.

More about this role

Professor of Psychiatry and Molecular Medicine at the Donald and Barbara Zucker School of Medicine at Hofstra/Northwell; former long-standing Chair of Psychiatry at Zucker Hillside Hospital. Affiliation listed for identification only — not an institutional endorsement of this website or any financing vehicle.

His published company role is leadership of the schizophrenia programme. First-episode and schizophrenia treatment research informs psychiatric development generally; it does not by itself establish CHR-P prevention claims for NWPT-SM32300.
