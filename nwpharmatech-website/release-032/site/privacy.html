Privacy notice

This notice covers the public informational website at nwpharmatech.org. It is not a full corporate privacy policy for clinical trials or patient care.

What this site collects

We do not run an account system or payment system. The “Explore a research collaboration” page keeps your planning notes in your browser only, as described below. Online enquiries are not active; contact is by email.

If you email team@nwpharmatech.com, your message is processed as ordinary business email (including standard mailbox security and retention practices of our email provider). Please do not send personal health information, clinical records, or patient-identifiable data.

Cookies and analytics

This public site is intended to operate without advertising cookies. Hosting may create technical logs (for example IP address, user agent, and request time) needed for security and reliability.

Research collaboration planning tools

The “Explore a research collaboration” page lets you answer optional questions, build an agenda of topics and write your own questions. Your optional answers, agenda and questions are stored in your browser for this tab’s session. Browser session-restoration features may preserve them. You can clear your answers and remove agenda items using the page controls. These planning contents are not sent to NWPharmaTech.

To show whether online enquiries are available, the page makes one extra request to this website. That request contains none of your answers, agenda items or questions.

Links you copy from the page contain only the page’s content version and the identifiers of the public topics and cards you selected. They never include your answers, your questions or any contact details.

Discussion briefs are created in your browser. Printing a brief or saving it as a PDF does not send it to us.

Programme updates and online forms

On the public production website these routes stay email-first. The pages say so when the online form is off, and nothing is sent from the page in that state. Research-collaboration enquiries on “Explore a research collaboration” stay on that same hold.

A preview deployment can switch on two separate forms: programme updates, and an exploratory financing enquiry. Both stay off unless the preview is explicitly switched on and the email service and the security check are configured. A missing setting is a refusal, not a success. Copying those settings onto www.nwpharmatech.org does not switch the forms on.

Controller. NWPharmaTech Ltd, for this informational website. Privacy questions: team@nwpharmatech.com. Final mailbox routing is still for Filipp Korentsvit to set before any production activation. The preview note for a financing enquiry is Filipp.korentsvit@nwpharmatech.com, with team@nwpharmatech.com remaining the press and general address. That routing is not a production setting. This notice is not a legal sign-off.

Register for programme updates

Purpose. Occasional programme news you asked for. News only. Not a financing enquiry, not an investment waitlist, and not a request for health information. The updates form does not ask you to express interest in financing, and the financing form does not require this registration.

The form asks for your email address and, if you choose, your name, plus consent. You are not registered until you open the confirmation link in the email. The on-page message means the email service accepted the message for delivery. It is not proof that the message reached an inbox.

Processors. Resend sends the message and holds the programme-updates contact, including the opt-in record. Cloudflare Turnstile is the anti-abuse check. Turnstile may process technical signals such as IP address and browser or device signals. A submission is refused when that check cannot be verified. Resend and Cloudflare publish their own privacy terms for that processing.

The confirmation email contains an unsubscribe link. Using it opts that address out of the programme-updates topic at Resend, so later programme-update sends on that topic are not sent to it. The page says so only after Resend shows the address as opted out. A financing enquiry is separate one-off mail and is not stopped by that link.

Retention. This website does not keep its own subscriber database. Resend keeps the contact record, including whether the address is pending, confirmed, or unsubscribed. To ask for the contact to be removed, use the unsubscribe link or email team@nwpharmatech.com with the subject Privacy. This notice does not set Resend’s own backup retention.

The Resend contact needs a property named nwpt_updates and a programme-updates topic before the preview can record confirmation and unsubscribe. Until those exist, the form fails closed and does not claim that anyone was registered.

Express interest in programme financing

Purpose. An exploratory, non-binding conversation about programme financing. It is not an offer, not an allocation, and not a payment. You cannot invest, pay, or connect a wallet on this website.

The form asks for your name, email address, an optional organisation and role, and your message. It does not ask you to register for programme updates, and it has no wallet, payment, or allocation field.

The same processors apply: Resend accepts the one-off message for delivery; Cloudflare Turnstile checks the submission. The reply-to address is the email address you typed. Acceptance on the page means Resend accepted the message for delivery. It does not mean the message reached an inbox, and it does not mean you have invested, paid, subscribed, or been allocated anything.

Retention. Financing enquiries are one-off mail. This website does not keep a separate copy. The message remains in the recipient mailbox. To ask for deletion, email team@nwpharmatech.com with the subject Privacy and the reference from the confirmation, if you have one. How long that mailbox keeps mail is the mailbox provider’s practice. This notice does not promise a fixed deletion time.

Financing and document uploads

This public website does not accept investments, subscriptions, payments, wallet connections, or document uploads for diligence. Do not send financial account details by email.
