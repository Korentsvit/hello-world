Privacy notice

This notice covers the public informational website at nwpharmatech.org. It is not a full corporate privacy policy for clinical trials or patient care.

What this site collects

These pages are static. We do not run an account system, payment system, or open contact form that stores messages on this website.

If you email team@nwpharmatech.com, your message is processed as ordinary business email (including standard mailbox security and retention practices of our email provider). Please do not send personal health information, clinical records, or patient-identifiable data.

Cookies and analytics

This public site is intended to operate without advertising cookies. Hosting may create technical logs (for example IP address, user agent, and request time) needed for security and reliability.

Programme updates and online forms

Today, contact is by email only. There is no public email-registration form on this website.

When programme-updates registration goes live, the form will collect your email address, and your name if you choose to provide it, so that NWPharmaTech can send the occasional programme news you requested. The form will also record your consent and use a double opt-in confirmation step. No programme updates will be sent until you confirm through the email link.

The form will use Buttondown as the email service provider (confirmation, delivery, unsubscribe) and Cloudflare Turnstile as an anti-abuse check (technical request signals such as IP address and browser/device signals may be processed to verify a submission). A submission may be refused when the check cannot be verified. Provider privacy terms apply to their processing. We will not use website forms to accept investments, payments, wallet addresses, or patient health information.

Financing and document uploads

This public website does not accept investments, subscriptions, payments, wallet connections, or document uploads for diligence. Do not send financial account details by email.
