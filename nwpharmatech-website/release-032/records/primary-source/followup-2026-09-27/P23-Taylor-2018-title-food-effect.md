## Food-effect passage (verbatim + location)

### Abstract Results — journal **p. 1053**

> A high-fat meal increased CBD plasma exposure (Cmax and AUCt) by **4.85-** and **4.2-fold**, respectively; there was no effect of food on tmax or terminal half-life.

### Results §3.5 “Effect of Food on CBD PK” — journal **pp. 1061–1063**

> Following a high-fat breakfast, there was a significant increase in Cmax for CBD of **4.85-fold (90% CI 4.01–5.87)**, and a similar increase in AUCt of **4.2-fold (90% CI 3.63–4.85)**.

> There was no obvious effect of food on tmax of CBD … [nor] obvious effect of food on the t½,z of CBD or its metabolites.
