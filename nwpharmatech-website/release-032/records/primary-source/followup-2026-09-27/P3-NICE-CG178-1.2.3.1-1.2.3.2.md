**1.2.3 Treatment options to prevent psychosis**

---

## Recommendation **1.2.3.1** (verbatim; PDF Page 10 of 38) — both bullets

> **1.2.3.1** If a person is considered to be at increased risk of developing psychosis (as described in recommendation 1.2.1.1):
>
> - offer individual cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) with or without family intervention (delivered as described in the section on how to deliver psychological interventions) **and**
> - offer interventions recommended in NICE guidance for people with any of the anxiety disorders, depression, emerging personality disorder or substance misuse. **[2014]**

---

## Recommendation **1.2.3.2** (verbatim; PDF Page 10 of 38) — both bullets

> **1.2.3.2** Do not offer antipsychotic medication:
>
> - to people considered to be at increased risk of developing psychosis (as described in recommendation 1.2.1.1) **or**
> - with the aim of decreasing the risk of or preventing psychosis. **[2014]**
