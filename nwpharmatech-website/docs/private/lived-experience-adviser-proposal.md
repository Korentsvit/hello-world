# Proposal: paid lived-experience advisory role (private, for management)

**Status:** proposal only. No one has been appointed. Nobody is listed on the website until someone is appointed and consents.

## Purpose
Bring the perspective of people with lived experience of mental health difficulties, including early psychosis or being at clinical high risk, and of family members, into how the programme communicates and how studies are designed.

## Remit
- Review public content (the families guide, the Q&A, study pages) for clarity, tone and stigma.
- Advise on participant-facing study materials (information sheets, consent, the burden of visits) before ethics submission.
- Contribute to study design discussions: acceptability, outcomes that matter, and the burden of blood tests.
- Advise on how results are communicated to participants and the public.
- **Excluded:** investment or financing matters, promotion of any product, and making clinical decisions.

## Terms
- A paid role at a published rate, for example the NIHR payment guidance for public involvement, with expenses covered and time agreed in advance.
- A fixed term (for example 12 months), renewable, with clear hours and a named contact.
- Two or more advisers, or a small panel, so no single person represents "lived experience".

## Consent and safeguarding
- Written agreement covering the role, payment, confidentiality, and how contributions are credited. Advisers can choose to be anonymous or named.
- Separate, optional consent for any public profile, photograph or interview, which can be withdrawn at any time.
- No requirement to disclose personal health details. Support is available if discussions are distressing, and there is a clear route to raise concerns.
- Advisers must not be current study participants or their family members, to avoid conflicts. Any financial interest in the programme must be declared.
- No contact from, or with, the financing or investor workstream.

## Recruitment
Open recruitment through established mental health involvement networks and charities, with an accessible application and paid interview time.

## Decisions needed
Budget, number of advisers, reporting line, and the payment rate policy.
