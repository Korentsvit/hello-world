## Title rules

- **confirmed:** management has confirmed the title in writing. None yet.
- **records-agree:** two or more company records give the same title, at least one dated 2025–2026, and no record conflicts with it. Where the wording differs in detail, only the shared part is shown (Cannon, Jones). Shown on staging; blocks production until confirmed.
- **unconfirmed:** records conflict, the title rests on one record, or none is recorded. No title is shown, only a descriptor (a field of expertise or remit the records support). Blocks production.
- The August 2026 teaser can only corroborate another record. It never settles an appointment by itself, and it corroborates neither chair title because it names two chairmen.
