# Publication policy

**Status: proposal for management adoption.** Not adopted, and not shown on the public site. No timetable in it is a commitment.

Moved from the public study page (`study.html#publication-policy`, labelled "Draft for management adoption") in the correction release of 23 September 2026. Until it is adopted, public pages say only what the records support: Phase 1 results are not yet published, a results summary is planned for both registries and the site, and the Phase 2B study will be registered before anyone is enrolled.

## Proposed text

1. **Disclosure regardless of outcome.** Results of every study of NWPT-SM32300 would be made public whether they are positive, negative or inconclusive, including studies stopped early.
2. **Registry results.** Summary results would be posted on each registry where the study is registered, within the period the registry or applicable law requires.
3. **Plain-language summary.** A plain-language summary would be published on this site and offered to participants, reviewed for accuracy and readability before release.
4. **Journal submission.** Full results of each Phase 2 or later study would be submitted to a peer-reviewed journal, with authorship decided by recognised authorship criteria and no funder or investor right to delay or alter the manuscript.
5. **Timing.** The proposed timings for each step are to be set by management when the policy is adopted.

## Decisions for management

- Adopt, amend or reject each point, and set the timings in point 5.
- Name who approves each release of results.
