# Editorial standards

**Status: proposal for management adoption.** Not adopted, and not shown on the public site.

Moved from the public newsroom (`newsroom.html#standards`, labelled "Draft for adoption") in the correction release of 23 September 2026. The corrections log itself stays public on the newsroom and evidence library pages.

## Proposed text

Proposed standards for content on this site.

- **Scientific review.** A qualified scientific reviewer checks each clinical or scientific statement against its source, confirms the evidence label, and checks that findings from other products are not presented as findings for NWPT-SM32300.
- **Editorial review.** An editor checks clarity, accessibility, plain language and consistency with the shared content source, and that nothing reads as promotion of an unapproved medicine.
- **Publication approval.** A named approver, who is not the author, signs off publication. Content about funding or investment also needs legal approval before publication.

Also proposed:

- Every update carries the date it was published; reviews are never backdated.
- When something changes, a new dated update is added rather than earlier ones being rewritten (removed from the updates page for the same reason).
- Corrections are recorded in the corrections log.
- Programme updates and reports are not investment communications.

## Decisions for management

- Adopt, amend or reject each standard.
- Name the scientific reviewer, the editor and the approver (roles, or people once appointed). Do not list anyone publicly until they have been appointed.
