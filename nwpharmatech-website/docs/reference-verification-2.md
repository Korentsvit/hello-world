## Corrections to the brief

1. Taylor 2018: the title does not contain "GW42003". Use the full "Highly Purified Cannabidiol" title.
2. Kane 2016 was published in the **American Journal of Psychiatry**, not NEJM.
3. The JAMA Psychiatry 2021 paper reports both meta-analytic pooled estimates and Kaplan-Meier estimates, and the numbers differ (for example, 3 years: 25% pooled vs 27% KM). State which set is used.
4. Stafford 2013 CBT effect: RR 0.54 at 12 months (not 0.64).
