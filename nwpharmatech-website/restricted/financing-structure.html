Programme financing structure

Development objective: platform-integrated programme financing, potentially including a permissioned token at closing, with register-first as the fallback.

1. Preferred route: platform-integrated programme financing

  1. Programme vehicle. A dedicated entity holds the economic interest in the NWPT-SM32300 programme. [TBC: entity type, jurisdiction and relationship to NW PharmaTech Ltd]
  2. Regulated platform. Investor onboarding, eligibility classification and KYC/AML run on a regulated investor platform integrated with the vehicle's records. [TBC: platform provider]
  3. Closing. At closing, interests are recorded on the vehicle's register and may be issued as permissioned tokens whose transfer rules enforce holder eligibility.
  4. Reporting. Holders receive periodic programme and financial reporting; clinical data and decisions are excluded.

2. Fallback: register-first

Interests are issued and recorded on a conventional register at closing. A tokenised representation may be added later only if lawful, approved and in holders' interests. The register remains the legal record of ownership in both routes. [TBC: counsel to confirm]

3. Firewall between financing and clinical decisions

  • No investor rights over study design, eligibility, dosing, safety, analysis or publication.
  • No access to participant data.
  • No solicitation of study participants or families; no use of recruitment or clinical channels.
  • Separation of scientific communications from any financial promotion.

4. Items not carried forward from earlier drafts

The October 2025 draft included projected returns, liquidity timelines, market-size claims and a retail utility token. These are excluded from this draft pending counsel review, because they are forward-looking financial statements or potential promotions.
