Research programme · Clinical high risk of psychosis

Rigorous research for people at clinical high risk of psychosis

NWPharmaTech is developing NWPT-SM32300, an investigational oral cannabidiol formulation, and planning a Phase 2B study to evaluate dose response, symptoms, safety and tolerability. Whether it works is not yet known. That is the question our research is designed to answer.

How to read this website

We label what is known and what is not

Research communication is only useful if it is honest about uncertainty. Throughout this site, scientific statements carry one of three labels.

Established

Supported by consistent evidence, systematic reviews or clinical guidelines, and broadly accepted in the field.

Emerging evidence

Reported in early or small studies. Promising or informative, but not confirmed and may not hold up in larger trials.

Open question

Not yet known. These are the questions our research, or future research, aims to answer.

Our approach

Pharmaceutical rigour, open communication

We aim to connect careful pharmaceutical development with transparent public communication. In practice this means:

  • Clear evidence standards. We separate established findings from hypotheses, and cite our sources.
  • Independent clinical decisions. Decisions about study design, participant safety, data analysis and publication rest with the clinical and scientific leadership and independent oversight bodies, not with funders or investors.
  • Reporting all results. We intend to register the study publicly before it starts and to report its results whatever they show. [CONFIRM: registry and publication policy.]
  • Dated updates. Programme news is published with dates, so you can see what has changed and when.

Important

NWPT-SM32300 is investigational. It is not approved by any medicines regulator and its effectiveness is unproven.

Cannabidiol products sold in shops or online are not the same as NWPT-SM32300, and they have not been shown to prevent psychosis.

If you are worried about your mental health, please speak to your GP or a mental health professional. Read our Q&A.

Programme financing

Exploring broader access to research financing

Alongside the clinical programme, we are developing a financing route through which eligible investors, which might include people in the crypto and mental-health communities, could participate via a dedicated programme vehicle, potentially using permissioned tokens.

This work is at an early stage and subject to legal and regulatory review. Nothing on this website is an offer of any investment. Financing arrangements will have no influence on clinical decisions.
