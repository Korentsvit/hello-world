Programme updates

Programme updates

Dated events that have happened. Planned steps are listed separately on the study page. When something changes, we add a dated entry rather than rewriting earlier ones.

Timeline

  1. 30 March 2026 · Operational

    Phase 1 analysis outputs delivered

    The statistical and pharmacokinetic analysis outputs for the Phase 1 study were delivered. A results summary will be published after review.

  2. 11 March 2026 · Clinical

    Phase 1 database locked

    The Phase 1 study database was locked so that the final analysis could begin.

  3. 10 December 2025 · Clinical

    Phase 1 study completed

    The last study activity took place in the Phase 1 study of NWPT-SM32300 in 14 healthy volunteers.

  4. 27 August 2025 · Clinical

    Phase 1 study started

    The first healthy volunteers gave consent to take part in the UK Phase 1 study (NCT07186283), which compared single doses of NWPT-SM32300 with the licensed cannabidiol medicine Epidyolex.

About these updates

Each entry records a completed event and is supported by a programme record.

Updates are written for the public, patients and clinicians. They are not investment communications.
