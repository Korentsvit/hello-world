Programme updates

Milestones

Every entry is dated. Planned steps are marked as planned. When plans change we add a dated note rather than rewriting earlier entries.

Timeline

  1. Planned

    Phase 2B study: regulatory and ethics submissions

    The Phase 2B protocol is being finalised. The study will be submitted for regulatory and research ethics review, and registered publicly before anyone is enrolled.

  2. Planned

    Phase 1 results summary

    A plain-language summary of the Phase 1 results will be published here and on ClinicalTrials.gov after review.

  3. 11 March 2026

    Phase 1 database locked

    The Phase 1 study database was locked, allowing the final analysis to begin.

  4. 10 December 2025

    Phase 1 study completed

    The last study activity took place in the Phase 1 study of NWPT-SM32300 in 14 healthy volunteers.

  5. 27 August 2025

    Phase 1 study started

    The first healthy volunteers gave consent to take part in the UK Phase 1 study (NCT07186283), which compared single doses of NWPT-SM32300 with a licensed cannabidiol medicine, Epidyolex.

Our updates policy

We publish milestones when they happen, not forecasts presented as facts.

We report setbacks and delays as well as progress.

Updates are written for the public, patients and clinicians. They are not investment communications.
