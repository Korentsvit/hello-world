Evidence & references

Supporting evidence

The sources behind the statements on this website. We cite peer-reviewed research and clinical guidelines, and we label how strong the evidence is.

How we label evidence

Established

Consistent findings across studies, systematic reviews or meta-analyses, regulatory decisions or clinical guidelines.

Emerging evidence

Findings from individual, small, short or experimental studies. Informative, but not confirmed, and sometimes contradicted by other studies.

Open question

Questions that current evidence cannot answer, including the questions our programme aims to address.

Labels are reviewed by the programme's clinical and scientific team when content is published or updated. If you think we have mislabelled something, please tell us.
