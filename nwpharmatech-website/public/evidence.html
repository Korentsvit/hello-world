Evidence & references

Supporting evidence

The published sources behind this website, and how we label the strength of what they show.

How we label evidence

Established

Consistent findings across studies, systematic reviews or meta-analyses, regulatory decisions or clinical guidelines.

Emerging evidence

Individual, small, short or experimental studies. Informative, not confirmed, and sometimes contradicted by other studies.

Programme information

Facts about NWPT-SM32300 and our own studies, from programme records and trial registries. Not evidence of benefit.

Open question

Questions current evidence cannot answer, including those our programme aims to address.

One label applies to each section or study summary. Labels are reviewed by the programme's scientific team whenever content changes. If you think something is mislabelled, please tell us.
