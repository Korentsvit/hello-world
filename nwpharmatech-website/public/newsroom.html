About us

NWPharmaTech Ltd is a UK company developing NWPT-SM32300, an investigational oral cannabidiol medicine, for people at clinical high risk of psychosis. A Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers (NCT07186283) was completed in 10 December 2025; a Phase 2B study is planned. NWPT-SM32300 is not approved for any use and its effectiveness is unproven.

When writing about the programme

Please describe NWPT-SM32300 as investigational, and do not describe it as a treatment or cure. Results from other cannabidiol products are not results for NWPT-SM32300.

How we report progress

Each programme report is written to answer four questions:

  1. What changed? Events since the last report, with dates.
  2. What supports it? The record or source for each statement.
  3. What remains unresolved? Open questions, delays and risks.
  4. What happens next? The next steps, marked as planned.

Individual events are listed on the updates page as they happen.

Corrections log

No corrections have been made since publication.

When we correct a substantive error in published content, we record the date, what was wrong and what we changed. Typographical fixes are not listed.

Editorial standards

Draft for adoption

Proposed standards for content on this site. They will apply once adopted by management.

Scientific review

A qualified scientific reviewer checks each clinical or scientific statement against its source, confirms the evidence label, and checks that findings from other products are not presented as findings for NWPT-SM32300.

Editorial review

An editor checks clarity, accessibility, plain language and consistency with the shared content source, and that nothing reads as promotion of an unapproved medicine.

Publication approval

A named approver, who is not the author, signs off publication. Content about funding or investment also needs legal approval before publication.

  • Every update carries the date it was published; reviews are never backdated.
  • Corrections are recorded in the log above.
  • Programme updates and reports are not investment communications.
