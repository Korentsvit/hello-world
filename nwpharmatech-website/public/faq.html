About the treatment

Does NWPT-SM32300 work?

We do not know. NWPT-SM32300 is an investigational medicine and its effectiveness is unproven. The completed Phase 1 study looked at safety and how the body handles it, not at whether it works. The planned Phase 2B study is designed to provide the first evidence on dose response, symptoms, safety and tolerability.

Can I get NWPT-SM32300?

No. It is available only within approved clinical research, and the Phase 2B study is not recruiting. We cannot supply it on request.

Should I take shop-bought CBD to lower my risk of psychosis?

Please talk to a doctor first. There is no evidence that CBD products sold in shops or online prevent psychosis or help people at clinical high risk. Their content and quality vary, some contain THC, and CBD can interact with other medicines.

Isn't cannabis linked to psychosis?

Frequent use of high-THC cannabis is associated with a higher risk of psychosis. Cannabidiol (CBD) is a different compound that does not produce a "high". Whether CBD helps in psychosis is still being researched. See the science.

What is in the capsule?

Each NWPT-SM32300 softgel contains 300 mg of highly purified cannabidiol in a self-emulsifying formulation. The capsule shell contains gelatine of bovine origin, which may matter for dietary or religious reasons. Full ingredients will be listed in participant information for any study.

About taking part

Is the Phase 2B study recruiting?

Not yet. It needs regulatory and ethics approvals before it can start. Recruitment details will be published on the updates page and on the public trial registry.

Who might be able to take part?

The study is planned for people at clinical high risk of psychosis, as assessed by specialist services. Full eligibility criteria will be published with the protocol summary.

Would taking part affect my usual care?

No. Participants would continue to receive their usual care throughout, and could leave the study at any time without it affecting their care.

About the programme

How is the programme funded?

The programme has been funded privately to date. We are developing a financing route for eligible investors; see programme financing. Funding does not influence clinical decisions.

Will you publish results if they are negative?

Yes. We will report results whatever they show, on the public trial registry and here.

Is this website medical advice?

No. It gives general information about a research programme and should not be used to make decisions about your own care. Always speak to a qualified health professional.
