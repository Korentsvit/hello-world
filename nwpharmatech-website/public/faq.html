About the investigational medicine

Does NWPT-SM32300 work?

We do not know. NWPT-SM32300 is an investigational medicine and its effectiveness is unproven. The completed Phase 1 study looked at safety and how the body handles it, not at whether it works. The planned Phase 2B study is intended to give the first evidence in people at clinical high risk, looking at dose response, symptoms, safety and tolerability.

Can I get NWPT-SM32300?

No. It is available only within approved clinical research, and the Phase 2B study is not recruiting. We cannot supply it on request.

Should I take shop-bought CBD to lower my risk of psychosis?

Please talk to a doctor first. There is no evidence that CBD products sold in shops or online prevent psychosis or help people at clinical high risk. They are not licensed medicines, and CBD can interact with other medicines. Source: Epidyolex SmPC

Isn't cannabis linked to psychosis?

Daily use of high-THC cannabis is associated with a higher risk of psychosis. Source: Di Forti et al. 2019 Cannabidiol (CBD) is a different compound that does not produce a "high". Whether CBD helps in psychosis is still being researched. See the science.

What is in the capsule?

Each NWPT-SM32300 softgel contains 300 mg of highly purified cannabidiol in a self-emulsifying (micellar) lipid formulation. The capsule shell contains gelatine of bovine origin, which may matter for dietary or religious reasons.

About taking part

Is the Phase 2B study recruiting?

Not yet. It needs regulatory and research ethics approval before it can start.

Who might be able to take part?

The study is planned for people at clinical high risk of psychosis. Its detailed eligibility criteria have not been published.

Would taking part affect my usual care?

Taking part in any study is voluntary, and anyone taking part can leave at any time without it affecting their usual care. How the Phase 2B study would fit alongside usual care will be set out in its participant information.

About the programme

Can I invest in the programme through this website?

No. A financing route for eligible investors is under evaluation, but no investment is being accepted through this website or through that route, and no investment, payment or token function on this website is active. What funding would support and who controls spending are explained under funding and financing. Clinical decisions are kept independent of funding.

Have the Phase 1 study results been published?

Results have not been published yet. See the Phase 1 study.

Is this website medical advice?

No. It gives general information about a research programme and should not be used to make decisions about your own care. Always speak to a qualified health professional.
