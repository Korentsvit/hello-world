NWPT-SM32300 and the planned Phase 2B study

Our programme centres on NWPT-SM32300, an investigational oral cannabidiol formulation, and a planned Phase 2B study in people at clinical high risk of psychosis.

Status: in planning, not yet recruiting

Investigational product

NWPT-SM32300 is not approved by any medicines regulator for any use. It is available only within authorised clinical research. Its effectiveness is unproven, and its safety in people at clinical high risk of psychosis is still being studied.

What "Phase 2B" means

Medicines are tested in stages. Phase 1 studies look mainly at safety and how the body handles a medicine. Phase 2 studies look at whether it may work in people with the condition, and at which dose.

A Phase 2B study typically compares several doses with a placebo to understand the dose–response relationship and to inform whether, and how, a larger Phase 3 study should be done.

What the study is designed to find out

1

Dose response

Whether different doses produce different effects, and which dose, if any, should be taken forward.

2

Symptoms

Whether NWPT-SM32300 reduces symptoms compared with a control group, measured with established clinical scales.

3

Safety

What side effects occur, how often, and whether any safety concerns arise, including in blood tests.

4

Tolerability

Whether people are able and willing to take it as intended, and how it affects daily life.

A Phase 2B study is not designed to prove that a medicine prevents psychosis. A positive result would justify further, larger studies; it would not on its own lead to approval.

Protecting participants

Participant safety and wellbeing come before every other consideration. The study is being planned to include:

  • independent review and approval by a research ethics committee and the relevant medicines regulator(s) before it starts;
  • informed consent, with time for questions and the freedom to withdraw at any time without affecting usual care;
  • continued access to standard clinical care throughout, alongside the study;
  • an independent Data Monitoring Committee that reviews safety data and can recommend changes to, or stopping of, the study; [CONFIRM]
  • regular safety monitoring, including blood tests for liver function and checks for interactions with other medicines.

Can I take part?

The study is not yet recruiting. We are not able to provide NWPT-SM32300 outside an approved study.

When recruitment opens, information on eligibility and study sites will be published here and on the public trial registry. Participants are usually referred through specialist mental health services.
