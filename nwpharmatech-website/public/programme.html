The programme

NWPT-SM32300 and the planned Phase 2B study

NWPT-SM32300 is an investigational oral cannabidiol medicine. A Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers is complete. A Phase 2B study in people at clinical high risk of psychosis is planned.

Phase 2B: planned, not recruiting

About NWPT-SM32300

Programme information
  • An oral softgel capsule containing 300 mg of highly purified cannabidiol.
  • A self-emulsifying (micellar) formulation, designed to carry a high dose of cannabidiol per capsule and to be absorbed consistently.
  • Formulation developed with the University of Toronto; manufactured to Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) standards by Thermo Fisher Scientific.
  • THC is not detected in the product at the sensitivity of the validated test method.
  • The capsule shell contains gelatine of bovine origin.

Investigational medicine

NWPT-SM32300 is not approved by any medicines regulator for any use and is available only within authorised clinical research. Whether it is effective is unknown, and its safety in people at clinical high risk of psychosis has not yet been studied.

What "Phase 2B" means

Medicines are tested in stages. Phase 1 studies look mainly at safety and how the body handles a medicine, usually in healthy volunteers. Phase 2 studies look at whether it may work in people with the condition, and at what dose.

A Phase 2B study typically compares more than one dose with a control to understand the dose–response relationship, and to decide whether a larger Phase 3 study is justified.

Phase 1 study: completed

Programme information

A study in healthy volunteers of how the body absorbs and handles NWPT-SM32300, and of its safety after single doses. It was not designed to test whether NWPT-SM32300 works.

What the study is designed to find out

Open question
1

Dose response

Whether different doses have different effects, and which dose, if any, to take forward.

2

Symptoms

Whether symptoms change more with NWPT-SM32300 than in the control group, on established clinical scales.

3

Safety

Which side effects occur and how often, including changes in blood tests such as liver function.

4

Tolerability

Whether people can take it as intended, and how it affects daily life.

A Phase 2B study cannot show that a medicine prevents psychosis. A positive result would justify larger studies; it would not on its own lead to approval.

Protecting participants

Participant safety comes first. The study will include:

  • approval by a research ethics committee and the relevant medicines regulators before it starts;
  • informed consent, time for questions, and the freedom to leave at any time without affecting usual care;
  • continued access to standard clinical care throughout;
  • regular safety monitoring, including blood tests of liver function and checks for interactions with other medicines;
  • review of safety data during the study, with independent oversight where regulators require it.

Can I take part?

Not yet: the study is not recruiting. We cannot supply NWPT-SM32300 outside an approved study.

When recruitment opens, eligibility and study sites will be published here and on the public trial registry. Participants are usually referred by specialist mental health services.
